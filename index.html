Get in touch

For help with the app, your login, or anything else about the club, email kcc@purelymail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I get a login?

Logins are for squad players. The captain sets one up for you and sends you your email and passcode. Anyone can see fixtures, results and stats without signing in.

I’ve forgotten my passcode

Email us and the captain will send you a new one.

When do availability replies close?

At 9pm Eastern time on the Wednesday before each game, or earlier if the side has already been picked. If your plans change after that, use “Email the captain” in the app.

Why can’t I change my reply?

Replies lock once they close so the captain can pick the side. Email the captain if you can no longer play.

How do I delete my data?

Email kcc@purelymail.com from the address you sign in with and we’ll remove your login and replies. See the privacy policy for details.
